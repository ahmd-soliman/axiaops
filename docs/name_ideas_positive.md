# Name Ideas — Positive Connotations Only

All names here carry themes of clarity, light, intelligence, efficiency, or discovery.
No ghost, waste, shadow, burn, or idle references.

---

## Clarity / Visibility Theme
- **Lucid** — clear thinking, full visibility into your cloud costs
- **Lens** — brings hidden costs into focus
- **Prism** — breaks down your cloud bill into its true components
- **Lumen** — unit of light, illuminate what was hidden
- **Vero** — Latin for "true" / "real" — see your real cloud spend
- **Klar** — German/Swedish/Norwegian for "clear" — works across EU markets

## Intelligence / Insight Theme
- **Sage** — wise, calm, trusted advisor
- **Keen** — sharp awareness, keen eye on your spend
- **Seren** — Welsh for "star," also serenity — calm clarity over your costs
- **Acumen** — sharp financial insight
- **Distil** — distil your cloud bill to what matters

## Discovery / Surface Theme
- **Scout** — finds what others miss
- **Beacon** — guides you toward savings
- **Reveal** — surface hidden costs
- **Surfaced** — exactly what the product does
- **Uncover** — action-oriented, positive

## Efficiency / Lean Theme
- **Trim** — lean and efficient
- **Cull** — selective precision (mild negative edge, borderline)
- **Refine** — continuously improve your cloud spend
- **Leaner** — cloud infrastructure, leaner
- **Tighten** — tighten your cloud spend

## Light / Energy Theme
- **Noor (نور)** — Arabic for light — founder's language, universal concept
- **Hikari (光)** — Japanese for light — respected in tech culture
- **Sol** — sun, clarity, energy — works in many languages
- **Aura** — glow of awareness around your infrastructure
- **Solus** — Latin for "alone/clean" — clean cloud, clean bill

## Nautical / Navigation Theme
- **Helm** — you are in control of your cloud costs
- **Vela** — Latin/Spanish for sail/candle — moving forward with light
- **Crest** — top of the wave, peak performance
- **Buoy** — keeps you afloat financially (borderline playful)

## Short / Abstract (SaaS-native)
- **Keto** — lean, efficient (known concept, no cloud conflict)
- **Vera** — true, real, trustworthy
- **Clio** — Greek muse of history/record — your cost history, recorded
- **Navi** — navigate your cloud costs (also: friendly guide)
- **Viso** — Italian/Esperanto for "face/vision" — see your costs clearly
- **Luno** — light-derived, modern, no baggage
- **Claro** — Spanish/Portuguese for "clear" — widely understood

## Shortlist — Best Overall

| Name | Origin | Meaning | Why it works |
|------|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Lucid** | English/Latin | Clear, aware | Instantly understood, professional, strong SaaS feel |
| **Noor** | Arabic (نور) | Light | Founder's language, universal concept, short and clean |
| **Lens** | English | Focus, clarity | Engineers love precision tools — a lens is a precision tool |
| **Klar** | Germanic | Clear | Works across Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Nordics |
| **Beacon** | English | Guide, light | Positive action — guides you to savings |
| **Sage** | English/French | Wisdom | Trusted, calm, advisor feel — good for B2B |
| **Scout** | English | Discover, find | Action-oriented, friendly, no negative edge |

---

## Top 3 Recommendation

1. **Noor** — short, one syllable, global concept (light exists in every culture),
   authentic to the founder's background, zero negative connotation in any language,
   strong domain potential.

2. **Lucid** — the most "SaaS-ready" name on the list. Instantly communicates
   clarity and intelligence. Works perfectly with a tagline like
   "Lucid — clear visibility into your cloud costs."

3. **Lens** — precise, technical, trusted. Engineers think of a lens as a tool
   that reveals detail. Pairs well with the product: "Lens shows you exactly
   where your cloud money goes."
