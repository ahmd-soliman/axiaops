# Name Ideas — FinOps Ghost Spend Detector

## Ghost / Phantom Theme
- **AxiaOps** — professional, one word, ghost-adjacent
- **Phantom** — clean, memorable, strong brand potential
- **Wraith** — dark, technical feel, unique
- **Shade** — minimal, modern, short
- **Haunt** — evocative, "your cloud costs are haunting you"
- **Revenant** — "the resource that came back to cost you money"

## Waste / Burn Theme
- **Burnout** — double meaning: cloud burn rate + burnout
- **Smolder** — slow burn, idle waste
- **Char** — short, sharp, burning reference
- **Ember** — warm, friendly, still implies slow burn

## Idle / Drift Theme
- **Drift** — resources drifting with no usage, also "cloud drift"
- **Idle** — literal, clean, SEO-friendly
- **Stale** — direct, instantly understood by engineers
- **Linger** — resources that linger after they should be gone
- **Dormant** — clear meaning, professional

## Visibility / Detection Theme
- **Unlit** — dark unused resources, brought to light
- **Surfaced** — "we surface hidden costs"
- **Lucent** — light/clarity, seeing what was hidden
- **Clarity** — simple, used in other spaces but strong

## Sharp / Minimal (SaaS-native feel)
- **Varro** — made-up, short, no baggage
- **Nullify** — "nullify your waste"
- **Zeroize** — aggressive, "bring costs to zero"
- **Trim** — action-oriented, simple
- **Curb** — "curb your cloud spend"
- **Cull** — "cull idle resources"

## Favorites (Shortlist)
1. **AxiaOps** — already in use in docs, strong
2. **Phantom** — most recognizable ghost reference, clean domain potential
3. **Drift** — works on multiple levels (idle drift, cloud config drift)
4. **Stale** — engineers immediately understand it, SEO-friendly keywords
5. **Wraith** — most distinctive, least likely to conflict with existing brands
