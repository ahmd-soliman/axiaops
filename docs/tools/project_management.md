# Project Management & Ticketing — Free Tools

## GitLab Issues (Recommended — Already Included)

Since you're already on GitLab, use its built-in issue tracker before reaching for an external tool.

**Free features:**
- Issues with labels, assignees, milestones, and due dates
- Issue Boards (Kanban-style)
- Epics (group issues into larger initiatives)
- Time tracking
- Linked issues and merge request references
- Issue templates

**When this is enough:** Solo founder or small team (1–5 people). GitLab Issues + a simple board covers everything a pre-revenue startup needs.

---

## Linear (Best External Option)

**Free tier includes:**
- Unlimited issues
- Unlimited members
- 1 team
- Cycles (sprints)
- Roadmap view
- GitLab / GitHub integration (auto-link issues to commits)

**Why Linear over Jira:**
- Significantly faster UI
- Keyboard-first — built for engineers
- Auto-closes issues when linked MR is merged
- Free tier is genuinely usable, not crippled

**Free tier limit:** 1 team, 250 issues. Enough for early-stage.

---

## Notion (Documentation + Light Project Management)

**Free tier includes:**
- Unlimited pages and blocks
- Unlimited file uploads (up to 5MB each)
- 7-day page history
- Collaborative editing
- Databases (used as a lightweight issue tracker or roadmap)

**Best use for AxiaOps:** Internal wiki, meeting notes, investor/customer pitch docs, research notes. Not recommended as a primary issue tracker — use GitLab Issues or Linear for that.

---

## Trello

**Free tier includes:**
- Unlimited cards
- Up to 10 boards per workspace
- Unlimited storage (10MB per file)
- Basic automation (Butler — 250 runs/month)

**When to use:** Simple Kanban if you prefer a visual board over GitLab Issues. Less useful once the team grows beyond 2–3 people.

---

## Comparison

| Tool | Best For | Free Limit |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **GitLab Issues** | Code-linked tickets, no extra login | Unlimited on free tier |
| **Linear** | Fast issue management, sprints, roadmap | 250 issues, 1 team |
| **Notion** | Docs, wiki, research | Unlimited pages |
| **Trello** | Simple Kanban | 10 boards |

**Recommendation for AxiaOps:** Start with **GitLab Issues** (zero friction, already set up). Switch to **Linear** when you have a co-founder or first hire who needs a cleaner project management experience.
