# AxiaOps — Name Candidates (Final Shortlist)

### Criteria
- One word, max 3 syllables
- Positive or neutral connotation in all major languages
- Not already a known brand in tech/SaaS/finance
- Trademarkable — distinctive enough to register in EU + US
- Easy to pronounce globally (no ambiguous spelling)

## Top 5 — Ranked

### 1. AxiaOps
Greek for "value" and "worth." A FinOps tool is fundamentally about understanding
the value of every cloud resource. Clean, short, two syllables, no brand conflicts
in the SaaS space, easy to say in any language.
Tagline: *"Know the value of every resource."*

### 2. Kairos
Ancient Greek for "the right moment" — the precise, opportune time to act.
In FinOps: catch waste at exactly the right moment before it compounds.
Well-known in philosophy and theology but unused in cloud tooling.
Strong, memorable, unique.
Tagline: *"Act on cloud waste at the right moment."*

### 3. Mizaan
Arabic for balance and scale — the same symbol used in financial accounting
and justice. Directly relevant to cost balance. Rare in Western tech brands,
authentic founder connection, visually strong (scales as a logo).
Tagline: *"Balance your cloud spend."*

### 4. Verus
Latin for "true" and "genuine." See your true cloud costs — not the
estimate, not the surprise bill, the real number.
Clean, professional, works across all EU languages.
Tagline: *"Your true cloud cost, always."*

### 5. Ziya
Turkish and Persian for light and glow. Rare as a SaaS brand in Western markets,
unlike Noor. Short, two syllables, easy to spell, authentic.
Tagline: *"Bring light to hidden cloud spend."*
